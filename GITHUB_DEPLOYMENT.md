## 🌐 Enable GitHub Pages (2 Minutes)

### Step 1: Go to Repository Settings
1. Visit: https://github.com/devsfolk/Intercon
2. Click **Settings** (top right)

### Step 2: Enable Pages
1. Scroll down to **Pages** section (left sidebar)
2. Under **Source**, select:
   - Branch: **main**
   - Folder: **/ (root)**
3. Click **Save**

### Step 3: Wait & Access
- GitHub will build your site (1-2 minutes)
- Your site will be live at:
  
  **https://devsfolk.github.io/Intercon/**
